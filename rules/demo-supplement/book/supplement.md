This is a supplement rather than a ruleset: it adds no rules of its own
and instead uses somebody else's, which it reaches by reference rather
than by copying. Every value it quotes from the demo rules is
interpolated out of that corpus's build outputs, so this document cannot
drift from rules it does not own.

Read it as a worked example of the corpus profile beside it. What each
part is demonstrating is said in a design note under it, and design
notes are kept out of the in-game snippets by the same mechanism that
keeps GM material out of the players' handout.
