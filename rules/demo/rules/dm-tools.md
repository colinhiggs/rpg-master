The Dungeon Master runs the world, adjudicates anything the rules do not
cover, and is the only participant who can advance the round.

## Authority in play

The Dungeon Master may move any token on the map, spawn creatures,
remove them, and set any creature's hit points directly. Players may
move only their own token, and may adjust only their own hit points —
this is a convenience for tracking, not permission to heal at will.

Advancing to the next combatant in the initiative order is the Dungeon
Master's call alone. See [[turn-order]] for what that order is and how
it is set.

## Adjudication

When the rules are silent, the Dungeon Master decides, and the decision
stands for the rest of the session. Write it down; if the same question
comes up twice, it belongs in the rules rather than in the ruling.
